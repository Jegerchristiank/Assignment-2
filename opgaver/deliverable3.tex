\subsection{Deliverable 3: Delta Sheet Summarising Differences Between Stated and Observed Practice}
\label{sec:deliverable3}

\paragraph*{\textbf{Task Requirement (Deliverable 3)}}
\textit{Compare Deliverables 1 and 2 and produce a delta sheet with missing events, extra events, different order, timing distortions, workarounds/coordination steps, and relabeling/ambiguity.}

The comparison maps interview activities (Axx identifiers) to observed events (Exx identifiers). A delta is registered when at least one of the following differs: event presence, order, timing, workaround behaviour, or activity interpretation. This follows the Chapter 27 logic of comparing observed traces with a prior process description to expose model limits and recurring exceptions \parenciteshort{guidehealthinformatics2026ch27}.

Appendix~\ref{app:part1_supporting}, Table~\ref{tab:d3_delta_sheet} contains the complete delta sheet. The six required categories are all represented:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Missing event: no distinct seated breakfast-and-schedule-review step.
  \item Extra event: roommate coordination appears only in observed data.
  \item Different order: bag packing occurs after breakfast preparation starts.
  \item Timing distortion: hygiene begins later than the interview baseline.
  \item Workaround/coordination step: key/lock issue triggers ad hoc messaging.
  \item Relabeling/ambiguity: \textit{breakfast} changes from sit-down meal to banana plus coffee-to-go.
\end{itemize}

These deltas operationalise the say-do gap, meaning the difference between described routine and performed routine. They also prepare the reflection in Deliverable 4 by showing where model assumptions break under real execution conditions.
