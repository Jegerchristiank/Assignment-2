\subsection{Deliverable 3: Delta Sheet Summarising Differences Between Stated and Observed Practice}
\label{sec:deliverable3}

\subsubsection{Comparison Method}
The comparison maps interview activities (A01-A09) to observed events in Deliverable 2. A delta was registered when at least one of the following differed: event presence, order, timing, workaround behavior, or interpretation of activity labels.

The complete delta sheet is placed in Appendix~\ref{app:part1_supporting}, Table~\ref{tab:d3_delta_sheet}. In the main report, the key deltas are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Missing event: no distinct seated breakfast-and-schedule-review step in observation.
  \item Extra event: additional roommate coordination event appears only in observed data.
  \item Different order: bag packing occurs after breakfast preparation begins.
  \item Timing distortion: hygiene block starts later than stated baseline.
  \item Workaround/coordination: missing lock triggers ad hoc borrowing via messaging.
  \item Relabeling/ambiguity: ``breakfast'' shifts from sit-down meal to banana plus coffee-to-go.
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Category Coverage}
All six required categories are documented in Table~\ref{tab:d3_delta_sheet}: missing events, extra events, different order, timing distortions, workarounds/coordination steps, and relabeling/ambiguity.

\subsubsection{Overall Interpretation}
The interview captures the baseline intent of the routine, while observation captures local adaptation under real constraints. The delta sheet therefore documents a clear and traceable say-do gap between knowledge-as-stated and knowledge-as-discovered.
