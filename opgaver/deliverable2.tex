\subsection{Deliverable 2: Event Log Representing the Observed Routine}
\label{sec:deliverable2}

The observation was conducted on February 20, 2026 (06:45-07:30), two days after the interview on February 18, 2026. This separation supports comparison between interview elicitation and situated execution.

As described in Chapter 27 and the course methods slides, \textit{observation} complements \textit{interviews} by capturing situated judgement and practical adaptations that are often missing from recollection-based accounts \parenciteshort{guidehealthinformatics2026ch27,zajac2026methodsknowing}. To keep this step explicit, a short observation guide was used (Appendix~\ref{app:part1_supporting}, Table~\ref{tab:d2_observation_guide}). Notes were split into \textit{descriptive notes} and \textit{reflexive notes}, and only descriptive notes were transcribed into the event log \parenciteshort{zajac2026methodsknowing}.

The full machine-readable event log is submitted as a separate CSV artifact with timestamps, event labels, and context notes. This CSV is the \textit{work-as-recorded} layer of the study.

The submitted CSV uses the following fields: \texttt{case\_id}, \texttt{event\_id}, \texttt{timestamp} (ISO datetime), \texttt{event\_label}, \texttt{actor}, \texttt{tool}, \texttt{context\_note}, and \texttt{related\_activity\_id} (A01-A09 or NA).

The observed event-sequence excerpt is provided in Appendix~\ref{app:part1_supporting}, Table~\ref{tab:d2_observed_excerpt}. Relative to the transcript-based baseline (Appendix~\ref{app:part1_transcript}, Table~\ref{tab:app_transcript_part1}, lines L02-L12 and L14-L18), the key comparison anchors are E03 (extra coordination), E09 (order shift), and E10 (workaround).

The observation confirms the same overall routine boundary as Deliverable 1, but includes practical variation and coordination not present in the interview baseline. In particular, one extra coordination event (E03), one order change (E09 relative to A04/A05), and one explicit workaround (E10) are central for the delta sheet in Deliverable 3. These events represent \textit{work-as-done} under actual constraints.
