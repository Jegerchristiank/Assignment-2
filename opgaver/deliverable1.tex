\subsection{Deliverable 1: Description of the Routine Based on the Interview}
\label{sec:deliverable1}

\subsubsection{Study Context, Scope, and Boundaries}
The study was conducted individually with one participant (the author). The chosen routine is a weekday morning routine.

The interview was conducted on February 18, 2026 as a self-recorded, open-question interview. The focus was on activity sequence, variation points, tools, and routine boundaries.

Routine boundary:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Start: alarm sounds.
  \item End: participant leaves home (door closes).
\end{itemize}

\subsubsection{Interview-Based Routine Model}
The participant described the morning as mostly stable with minor variation. The baseline flow is: wake-up, hygiene, dressing/packing, breakfast preparation, breakfast/schedule check, final checks, departure.

Reported variation points were: occasional snoozing, occasional breakfast substitution when time is tight, and occasional short key-search episodes.

\subsubsection{Assumptions Used for Modelling}
\begin{itemize}
  \item The model represents a weekday baseline (not weekend behavior).
  \item The participant is the only consistent human actor in the routine.
  \item Expected times are interview estimates and are treated as approximate.
\end{itemize}

These assumptions are made explicit to support direct comparison with the observed event log in Deliverables 2 and 3.
