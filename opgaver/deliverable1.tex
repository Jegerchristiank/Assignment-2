\subsection{Deliverable 1: Description of the Routine Based on the Interview}
\label{sec:deliverable1}

\paragraph*{Scope and evidence}
This Part I study is a single-case self-study with one participant (the author) and one routine (weekday morning preparation before leaving home). The interview was conducted on February 18, 2026 as a semi-structured self-interview.

Following Chapter 27, this step is treated as \textit{manual knowledge acquisition}: it captures \textit{knowledge-as-stated} (what the participant reports) and produces an initial \textit{work-as-imagined} model (how the routine is expected to unfold) \parenciteshort{guidehealthinformatics2026ch27,zajac2026methodsknowing}. Internal identifiers used in the report are summarised in Table~\ref{tab:abbreviation_key}.

The routine boundary is anchored in transcript evidence (Appendix~\ref{app:part1_supporting}, Table~\ref{tab:app_transcript_part1}, lines L21--L22):
\begin{itemize}
  \item Start: the alarm sounds.
  \item End: the apartment door closes when leaving.
\end{itemize}

The baseline sequence is grounded in lines L02--L12: wake-up, hygiene, dressing/packing, breakfast, final checks, and departure. Variation points (snooze, breakfast substitution, key search, occasional roommate coordination) are grounded in lines L14--L18.

\paragraph*{Participants, order, and modelling assumptions}
Table~\ref{tab:d1_activity_spec} presents the interview-based activity model, and Figure~\ref{fig:d1_dcr_graph} shows the corresponding interview-based DCR graph. In this context, interview-based DCR means a process model built from interview evidence rather than direct observation.

Assumptions used in Deliverable 1 are:
\begin{itemize}
  \item The model represents a weekday baseline, not weekend behaviour.
  \item The participant is the only consistent human actor in the routine.
  \item Expected times are recall-based estimates and therefore approximate.
\end{itemize}

Together, these assumptions keep scope explicit and make later comparison with observed evidence in Deliverables 2 and 3 auditable.
